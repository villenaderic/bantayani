# Image Generation Prompts

Reference prompts for generating BantayAni brand and interface assets. Adjust aspect ratio and style keywords to fit whichever image generation tool is being used.

## Logo and icon

Primary mark
"A minimal, modern logo icon for an agricultural satellite monitoring platform named BantayAni. Combine a stylized rice leaf or crop blade with a subtle satellite orbit or radar sweep arc around it. Flat vector style, two to three colors, deep agricultural green and warm gold accent, on a transparent background, suitable for a government technology platform, no text, no gradients, no shadows."

App icon variant
"A square app icon, rounded corners, flat vector illustration of a single crop blade wrapped by a thin orbital ring, deep green background with a gold crop symbol centered, minimal and clean, no text, suitable for a mobile app icon."

Monochrome variant
"A single-color version of a crop blade with a thin orbital arc around it, solid deep green fill, transparent background, for use as a favicon or watermark."

## Notes on style consistency

Keep every generated asset within the same restrained palette: deep agricultural green, warm gold, neutral gray, and white, with orange and red reserved only for severity or alert indicators, and blue reserved for verified or system information states. Avoid photorealistic renders of real satellite imagery, since those should only ever come from actual satellite data sources, never from a generated image.
